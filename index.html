How to play

  1. Two teams: Purple and Blue. Each team has 6 Romans on its Start row.

  2. Pick a Commander. Tap one of your Romans to make it your Commander. Keep it safe!

  3. Who starts? Both players roll. The higher roll goes first.

  4. Roll 1, 2 or 3: move one Roman exactly that many squares left or right.

  5. Roll 4, 5 or 6: march one Roman exactly that many squares forward. If the board ends first, it stops on the last row and turns around.

  6. Battle! Land on an enemy Roman and you both roll the dice. The higher number knocks the other Roman out. A tie means roll again.

  7. Romans can't swim. You can't stop on the water or the black rock, but you can jump over them and over other Romans.

  8. Win: knock out the other team's Commander!

Stuck? If no Roman can ever reach a Commander, or nobody battles for 40 turns, the team with more Romans left wins.
